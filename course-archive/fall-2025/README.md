# CIS I Fall 2025 course archive

A personal archive of the materials posted on the Fall 2025 schedule page of JHU EN.601.455/655 Computer Integrated Surgery I (<https://ciis.lcsr.jhu.edu/doku.php?id=courses:455-655:2025:fall-2025-schedule>), downloaded on 2026-10-05. File names are unchanged. All material belongs to Russell H. Taylor, the CIS I teaching staff and the cited authors; it is kept here for private study only and must be removed before this repository is ever made public.

Files already used by the project are not duplicated here: the PA3/PA4 and PA5 handouts, PA5 notes, PA general expectations and report guide (in `docs/handout/`), the point-pair and rigid 3D-3D lecture notes (in `docs/reference/`), and the PA3 to PA5 data (unzipped in `data/`).
